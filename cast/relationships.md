# Relationship Map — Questions, Not Canon

| Relationship | Current direction | To explore |
|---|---|---|
| Hero ↔ Antagonist | Justice-driven Hero opposes cold, powerful Villain; both survive | First meeting, nonlethal pauses, why Villain spares Hero, how respect/hatred evolve |
| Hero ↔ Friends | Journey-based, mutually loyal but imperfect | Conflicts, growth, separations, emotional turning points |
| Antagonist ↔ Council | Extraordinary authority with genuine fear and loyalty | Who is oldest; advisers' autonomy; whether any can challenge decisions |
| Antagonist ↔ Independent allies | Reciprocal deals honored, not petty betrayal | Conditions, territories, information, magical resources, war help |
| Hero ↔ Antagonist's allies | Usually lower-tier opponents first | Why fighting occurs; when direct Villain battle finally matters |
| Both leads ↔ romantic interests | Meaningful love lives | Separate individual identities, no rote tragedy |

Most useful current rivalry mechanisms are elaborated in [story/rivalry-and-conflict.md](../story/rivalry-and-conflict.md).
