# Love Interests and Romantic Stakes — NOT CANON

**Working preference:** Both main leads are attractive and may each have a meaningful **love life**. Romantic bonds should carry real agency, tenderness, difficult choices and consequences.

Hero: romance can grow along the journey without reducing the partner to someone who needs rescuing.

Antagonist: a current or past romantic attachment might reveal the compassionate side beneath his severe exterior. Do **not** assume his girlfriend, wife, sister or partner must die; tragedy is an option, not a requirement.

Names, timing, relationship arcs, deaths and endings are all undecided.
