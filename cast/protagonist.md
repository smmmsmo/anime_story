# Protagonist / Hero — NOT CANON

**Working age:** ~16–18 during main journey. From a lower-status or struggling family/background; charming/handsome and relatable. Born with a real but limited power, which develops through battles, training and experience. He is **not powerless or a beggar** and must not be written as submissively pleading for Villain's mercy.

## Moral agency

The Hero stands for justice, protects people and has a backbone. He can be angry, wrong, inexperienced or deeply hurt without being cowardly or passive. **Extreme stakes may drive him to a sincere attempt to kill the Villain**; he does not automatically want to kill at every encounter.

## Experience

The story's dominant lens is his journey: friends met organically; changing loyalties; meaningful personal losses; mysteries uncovered; love; setbacks; hard-won growth; plenty of dangerous fights. A confrontation with the Antagonist should not imply their raw power is equal.

## Open questions

Identity; specific origin; first personal goal; relationships/family; first power; why his path intersects the Villain; limits and irreversible choices.
