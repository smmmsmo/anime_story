# Hero's Friends and Journey Companions — NOT CANON

The protagonist gains **friends through lived experience**, not by recruiting an RPG team on command. Some travel together; others appear across regions, rejoin later, or grow separately. Relationships include support, rivalry, humor only when earned, ideological disagreements, romance, separation, trust and sacrifice.
