# Antagonist's Council & Command Hierarchy — NOT CANON

The Villain occupies the **apex**; his presence and history command fear, respect and loyalty. His elite council consists of **high-ranking, exceptionally powerful individuals**, each with their own personality and agency. Members may be warriors, tacticians, intelligence brokers, magical specialists, terrifying creatures, independent rulers or diplomats.

Illustrative (non-final) command structure:

1. **Antagonist** — strategic objectives, highest authority, seldom fights.
2. **Inner council** — supreme advisers and formidable direct agents; may each oversee a sphere.
3. **Lieutenants / subordinate commanders** — execute operations, lead exceptional units.
4. **Armies, networks and regional forces** — conduct most conquest or defense.
5. **Independent allies** — *not necessarily direct subordinates*. They may rule other realms with their own councils and forces, maintaining negotiated commitments.

An ally can ask for a kingdom, passage, specific person, information, rare items or support against another ruler. The Villain may agree when those concessions don't obstruct his objective. His reputation benefits when he **honors promises and avoids gratuitous double-crosses**. This does not mean all outsiders are safe from the deal's consequences.

Do not write powerful council members as faceless disposable henchmen. No members, power rankings, names, exact number or faction flags are decided.
