# Alternative Story Premises — ALL NOT CANON

Creator wants **5–10 distinct stories**, not a single default. Below are eight *independent and modifiable* starting seeds (mostly from prior explorations), not episode plans.

| ID | Working seed | Central moral collision |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **The Seventh Oath** (liked earlier, not selected) | Border protections maintained through secret innocent sacrifices; expose system vs protect millions |
| P2 | **The Crown of Ash** | Royal order abandons poor provinces to protect the capital; who is expendable? |
| P3 | **The Broken Treaty** | Nobles' magical deals extract invisible costs from powerless families; voiding contracts may unleash threats |
| P4 | **The Gilded Vein** | Kingdoms profit from magical ore draining life from mining villages |
| P5 | **The Nameless Frontier** | So-called monsters were once betrayed defenders; compassion clashes with hero's experience of their violence |
| P6 | **The Memory Tax** | Protections financed by stolen memories/identities of children; reclaiming lives can undo the protections |
| P7 | **The Sealed Paths** | Royal families monopolize magic awakening; breaking access barriers invites dangerous forces |
| P8 | **The Mercy War** | Healing cities requires transferring fatal harm to unwilling victims |

All require major rewriting to integrate the two young leads, a privileged antagonist's rise, council/army, Hero's principled opposition and believable conflict. They may be merged or rejected. **No winning premise chosen.**

See [rivalry-and-conflict.md](rivalry-and-conflict.md) for conflict mechanics that can work with *any* seed.
