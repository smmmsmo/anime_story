# Narrative Principles — NOT CANON

1. **Journey first:** chiefly Hero's perspective, earned growth, travel, friendship, romance, mysteries, errors and hard decisions.
2. **Villain significance ≠ Villain screen time:** much of his power-building decade stays unseen; viewers meet rumors, aftermaths, retellings and rare direct encounters.
3. **Escalate consequences:** Hero frequently fights meaningful opponents, including antagonistic council-linked forces. Villain's own combat should be almost mythic in rarity and intensity.
4. **Earn the aura:** composure, history, fulfilled threats and followers' behavior establish menace; avoid empty “aura 999” dialogue.
5. **Meaningful chains of command:** the Antagonist need not participate in every conquest. Powerful subordinate sends a lieutenant, whose own army executes the operation.
6. **Justice versus terrible means:** do not dilute Hero into a supplicant. Give both characters agency, real opposition and imperfect understanding.
7. **Relationships count:** real friends, councils, allies and romantic partners are people with their own goals.
8. **Survival with cost:** avoid killing either lead as a cheap climax; have real losses, reversals or damaged objectives where appropriate.
9. **Not rigid arcs:** seasons and episodes emerge from a chosen story and themes, not a preset five-phase template.
