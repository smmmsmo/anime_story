# High-Stakes Endings — OPTIONS ONLY

The creator currently wishes **both Hero and Antagonist to survive**. The Hero may genuinely attempt to kill when morally compelled; survival must still feel credible.

Ideas to explore (not decisions): Hero stops a ritual or campaign and cannot finish the stronger Villain; a costly escape/disappearance; Villain voluntarily abandons a tactical advantage due to his code; a rescue with a terrible price; the protagonists separate after an unresolved ideological clash.

**Do not** make one character die and resurrect by coincidence, remove all consequences, or force a mercy speech in which the Hero begs. A nonlethal *outcome* is not synonymous with a nonlethal *intention*.

The Antagonist's actions *after accomplishing or failing his objective* are entirely undecided.
