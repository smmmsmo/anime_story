# Instructions for All Story Collaborators and AI Agents

Read README.md and STATUS.md first; then read relevant topic files and development/decisions.md. This is a **creative exploration repository, not a fixed script or an approved story bible**.

## Non-negotiable collaboration process

1. **Nothing is finalized unless the creator explicitly says so.** Words such as "like," "prefer," "could," "suppose," and "maybe" are provisional. Even selected options remain reversible. Label uncertainty.
2. **Do not infer or invent canon.** Never assert that The Seventh Oath, seven kingdoms, Black Divide, a sister's death, exact ages, council members, artifacts, alliances, or an ending are decided.
3. Generate and compare multiple substantially different directions before choosing plot. The creator wants roughly **5–10 stories/ideas** explored, not premature commitment.
4. Dialogue with the creator in **moderate conversational Bangla with natural English terms**, avoiding overly formal Bangla. Be a co-creator, not a lecturer. Ask focused questions when useful; do not repeatedly demand all decisions.
5. Maintain the stated priority: **story, character, world-building and narrative only**. Do not shift into animation production, tools, rendering, visual design or release planning without invitation.
6. Preserve the nuanced character power balance, emotions, motivations, relationships, and survival preference detailed in cast/ and settings/.
7. The setting may contain supernatural phenomena, magic, angels/angel-like beings, stones, rituals, creatures, and entities, **but no God, gods, or deity-derived origin or worship as a real supernatural explanation**.
8. Central conflict can be **morally grey** without making the Hero passive or morally indifferent: Hero is justice-driven and may choose lethal force in extreme circumstances; the Villain is genuinely dangerous and can do evil things while retaining understandable convictions.
9. The Antagonist is **much more powerful than the Hero**, especially early on. He is calm, civil, rational and frighteningly self-possessed. He delegates ordinary wars; personal fights should be rare, consequential and extraordinary.
10. **Early survival motive clarified:** the Antagonist may ignore the Hero out of immense superiority and contemptuous *indifference*, not kindness, sympathy or hesitation. The Hero is to him like an insignificant ordinary peasant beneath the attention of someone operating on an entirely different scale. He has no need to soil his hands with a trivial opponent; delegates if the nuisance must be removed. This is a plausible *early* motivation, not unlimited plot armor: once the Hero poses a substantial threat, the rationale must change or disappear. At the extreme final confrontation, both may genuinely fight with intent to kill.
11. **The creator presently wants both main characters to survive the story's major conclusion.** Hero may sincerely try to kill the antagonist during an extreme confrontation; survival must be earned and plausible. The exact ending is unspecified.
12. The Villain's inner council, lieutenants, allied rulers/creatures and armies are distinct levels of authority. Powerful allies have independent interests and credible negotiated benefits; do not default to repeated betrayal.
13. Both Hero and Villain may have romantic relationships and emotional stakes. Protagonist's traveling companions are friends developed organically, not merely a party roster.
14. Maintain narrative focus mainly through Hero's journey. The Villain's decade of growth is largely offscreen, glimpsed through consequences, witnesses, rumors, sparse flashbacks and later revelations.
15. When making updates, modify the relevant topical document(s), add date/context to development/session-log.md, and change development/decisions.md **only for explicit finalizations**. Do not erase existing notes or rename files without leaving navigational links.

## Source of truth conventions

- \`STATUS.md\`: current *non-binding* preferences and open uncertainties.
- \`development/decisions.md\`: explicit canon approvals only; currently none.
- \`development/archive/\`: untouched older source notes for recovery.
- \`development/open-questions.md\`: unresolved issues.
- \`story/premise-candidates.md\`: competing stories, not episodes.

Do not invent episode numbers, detailed timelines, names or power ranks unless explicitly proposing a labeled option.
