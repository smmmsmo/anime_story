# Open Questions (Live Backlog)

## Premise, stakes and objectives
- Which of the 8+ story premises, or which new concept, is strong enough to choose?
- What does Hero seek at the start? How does that change?
- What concrete outcome does Antagonist ultimately want? What does he do **afterward**?
- How can rivalry ideas **1 + 2 + 4** be combined, if at all, without coincidences?

## Origin, age and ascent
- Did antagonist suffer family loss, betrayal, witnessed injustice, or something else near age 11?
- Is sister's death a credible proposal or too familiar a trope?
- Why was he able to survive and build terrifying power during ten years offstage?
- Is he 18, 21 or 27 when the core story begins? What age gap best serves story?

## Character and emotion
- Why does an overwhelmingly stronger, ruthless strategist refrain from killing Hero in a particular confrontation?
- What is his actual code of honor, and which deals would he refuse?
- Who are the independent people in his council; why do they follow him?
- Who are Hero's friends, rivals, teachers and lover(s)?
- What does meaningful romantic life look like for both leads?

## Power, politics, world
- How are abilities born, awakened, trained, granted, resisted and exhausted?
- What powers do rare artifacts and powerful creatures grant, under what costs?
- How are kingdoms/empires structured? How many?
- Which creatures and evil/dark factions are rational actors, rather than random monsters?
- How can Hero challenge Villain without equalizing their raw power unrealistically?

## Structure and climax
- How late/rare is first direct confrontation?
- When does hero genuinely choose lethal force?
- How do both survive without cheap plot armor?
- Where and how do discoveries/flashbacks reveal the antagonist's missing decade?

All questions are intended to stay open until explored or explicitly settled.
