# Hybrid Power System — Favorite Approach, NOT CANON

Potential interacting paths, rather than a single leveling formula:

1. **Innate potential:** Hero starts with some power; family lines (including royal) may have advantages, but don't guarantee supremacy.
2. **Training, combat and breakthroughs:** physical/magical mastery earned over time, through danger, skill and experience.
3. **Artifacts and rare practices:** ancient stones, weapons, texts, forbidden methods or rituals can enable unusual growth or abilities.
4. **Creatures, boss encounters and pacts:** beings stronger than many mortals may reward, grant, teach, bargain, mutate or lend abilities depending on conditions.
5. **Non-raw-power advantages:** allies, intelligence, artifacts, rare knowledge, economic resources, territory, political control and military organization.

The Antagonist becomes **overwhelmingly** more powerful than the Hero by earning and combining advantages during largely unseen years. He was **not** born omnipotent.

## Unresolved constraints (must be solved before serious battles)

- Costs, bodily/moral limits, rarity, training time, recovery, exhaustion, counter-techniques, how powers interact, fair power scaling.
- How a child of 11 can survive long enough to access dangerous opponents.
- How elite commanders, monsters and entire armies coexist without trivializing ordinary fighters.
- How Hero can eventually challenge Villain meaningfully *despite a genuine raw-power gap*, rather than suddenly matching him.
- Is ability growth genetic, awakening-based, ritual-granted, or a mixture?

Avoid automatic game-like boss rewards unless explicitly chosen.
