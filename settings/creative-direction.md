# Creative Direction — Working Preferences, NOT CANON

- Original **dark medieval fantasy**; long, serious, emotionally heavy animated saga.
- Epic journey-driven progress, world-building, mystery, war, politics, betrayal, adventure, brutal consequences, sorrow, romance and earned triumph.
- Inspirations in experience/tone, not copied plots: Attack on Titan, Black Clover, God of War, Jujutsu Kaisen, Castlevania, Overlord, Street Fighter; emotional stakes of South Indian cinema.
- Focus on Hero's discoveries and growth; Villain has equivalent narrative significance without equal scene count.
- Villain's *aura*: silent controlled entry can end discussion; the figure embodies both impressive heroic qualities and terrifying antagonist force. No routine tantrums, loud swagger or mindless thug mannerisms.
- Hero is proud, justice-oriented, courageous, expressive through action—not weak, constantly begging, or preordained to win.
- Characters: love lives, friendships, alliances, rivalries, grief, moral compromise, loyalty; neither lead is disposable.
- Villain uses council/armies for regular conflicts. Sparse personal combat at exceptionally high stakes; Hero may fight much more frequently.
- Prefer organic long-form arcs; **no fixed five-phase template**; don't draft episodes before a premise is chosen.
- **No God-related supernatural rules.** Magic, ancient beings, rare stones, rites, angel-like aesthetics can exist without deities.
- **No plot canon yet.** Brainstorm, compare, revise without forcing decisions.
