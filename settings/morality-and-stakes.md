# Morality & Stakes — NOT CANON

**Preferred: morally grey central conflict**, not an uncomplicated Black vs White universe. But the story is *not* morally weightless:

- **Hero** sincerely stands for justice, vulnerable people and protecting others. He may come to believe killing the Villain is necessary after atrocities escalate.
- **Antagonist** is a true danger capable of ruthless, villainous choices, with an understandable history and consistent code. His viewpoint may challenge the audience without morally excusing every action.
- Agreements may be honorable between two leaders yet unjust to people affected by the bargain.
- A shared desire to change the world may produce opposing methods, collateral harm and irreconcilable duties.
- The Hero can win by stopping a catastrophic action even if he cannot overpower the Villain directly.
- Both major leads are envisioned **alive** beyond major confrontations. Do not equate heroic resolve with mandatory murder or villain survival with weakness.

Tests for each major conflict: Why is each person doing this? What innocent party pays the cost? What decision can neither justify to the other? Who benefits?
