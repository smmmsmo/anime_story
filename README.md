# Anime Story — Creative Lab

An **exploratory workspace** for developing an original, mature, long-form fantasy animated series.

> **NOT CANON:** Nothing recorded in this repository is finalized. Every character, age, kingdom, power rule, origin, title, and plot premise remains open to revision or replacement.

## Creative direction

Dark medieval fantasy; emotionally intense, character-driven storytelling; a gradual journey of discovery; moral ambiguity, power struggles, mystery, loss, alliances, and wars.

The narrative mostly follows a young protagonist. An equally important, initially sympathetic but increasingly frightening antagonist rises from a privileged background and becomes extraordinarily powerful through choices, combat, negotiations, and dangerous relationships. The antagonist's years of ascent need not be shown chronologically or at length.

**No gods or deity-based mythology.** Supernatural phenomena, magical systems, rare artifacts, rituals, creatures, and angel-like beings can exist without being gods.

## Working agreement

- Explore multiple ideas before choosing a direction.
- Treat "The Seventh Oath" as one possible starting point, not the final plot.
- Do not assume a fixed five-phase structure.
- Focus on writing and world-building; production, visual design, and animation technique come later.
- **Nothing is final until explicitly confirmed.**
