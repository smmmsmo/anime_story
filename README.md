# Anime Story — World, Cast & Story Development

> **EXPLORATION ONLY — NOTHING IS CANON.** This is a working creative repository, not a finalized story. All names, ages, world rules, plots, character traits, scene ideas and ending mechanisms can change unless the creator explicitly confirms them. No story is selected yet.

An original, dark, emotionally intense long-form fantasy animated saga in development. The immediate focus is **story, characters, world, and narrative**—not animation production.

## Start here (especially for a new chat or collaborator)

1. Read **[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md)** for how to collaborate and preserve the creator's intentions.
2. Read **[STATUS.md](STATUS.md)** for current preferences vs undecided questions.
3. Read **[settings/creative-direction.md](settings/creative-direction.md)** for the project's tonal and storytelling rules.
4. Read the topic-specific files below; consult **[development/session-log.md](development/session-log.md)** for discussion history.
5. Always check **[development/decisions.md](development/decisions.md)** before treating anything as finalized.

### Current big creative questions

- Which of multiple story premises best supports both Hero and Villain?
- What forces them to clash without a contrived grudge?
- What causes the privileged antagonist's childhood transformation, and what are his eventual objectives?
- How does the protagonist acquire power sufficient for a meaningful final confrontation without diminishing the antagonist's vast advantage?

**Previous "The Seventh Oath" outline is one liked candidate, not the adopted plot.** Full earlier notes are preserved in [development/archive/2026-10-22-initial-story-lab.md](development/archive/2026-10-22-initial-story-lab.md).

## Update practice

Put new character information in cast/, world content in environment/, governing rules in settings/, narrative options in story/, and only genuinely planned episodes in episodes/. Log major shifts in development/session-log.md. Never silently promote a brainstorm to canon.
