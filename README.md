### Current big creative questions

- Which of multiple story premises best supports both Hero and Villain?
- What forces them to clash without a contrived grudge?
- What causes the privileged antagonist's childhood transformation, and what are his eventual objectives?
- How does the protagonist acquire power sufficient for a meaningful final confrontation without diminishing the antagonist's vast advantage?

## Update practice

Put new character information in cast/, world content in environment/, governing rules in settings/, narrative options in story/, and only genuinely planned episodes in episodes/. Log major shifts in development/session-log.md. Never silently promote a brainstorm to canon.
