# Factions, Institutions & Alliances — NOT CANON

Types to consider: royal armies; frontier defense orders; wizard/warrior training guilds; independent kingdoms; corrupt institutions; creature domains; powerful rogue commanders; knowledge brokers; the Antagonist's inner council; and independent allied rulers.

## How alliances might work

**Not every ally shares the Villain's ideology.** A lord may want a province, an archivist forbidden knowledge, a creature protected territory, another ruler military support. The Antagonist might trade resources or territory he doesn't want to reach his own objective.

Agreements can yield troop support, **intelligence, magical enhancements, passage, supplies, territorial concessions, political recognition and coordinated warfare**. The Villain should not habitually betray cooperative allies; trust and a record of keeping his word make his power more credible.

**Moral stakes:** honoring a treaty doesn't absolve harm inflicted on innocent people. The Hero may resist the effects of a deal rather than the Villain's raw presence.

Never assume all allies are hierarchical subordinates; some are sovereign peers strategically aligned with him.
