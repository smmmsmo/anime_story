# Kingdoms, Thrones & Empires — NOT CANON

Possible political structures: multiple independent kingdoms; a large empire supervising vassal states; rival dynasties; frontier military provinces; royal family councils; independently ruled dark territories.

Some protagonists may have lower-class origins while the Antagonist hails from wealthy/royal lineage. Royal status may give better education, training, access to power, weapons, archives and political reach—but **does not grant final-level power for free**.

Important dilemmas: whether protection justifies sacrifice; who benefits from supernatural defenses; what rulers owe common citizens; negotiated cession of territory in war; what it means to honor a bargain that hurts innocents.

No fixed number of kingdoms or rulers; no approved political map.
