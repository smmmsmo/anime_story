# Society & Everyday Life — Working Direction

Updated 2026-10-22.

The user wants a **lived-in fictional anime fantasy world**, not a collection of constant battles or historically literal nation-states.

- Ordinary people continue daily life: farming, selling goods, raising families, playing, studying, traveling, practicing crafts and working in towns and villages.
- Magic, wild creatures, trained fighters and demon threats exist, but not every town is under daily attack. Protected settlements, local forces, escorts and safer routes make normal life possible.
- Each kingdom has its own people, politics, warrior traditions, class structures, economy and specialty without reducing the entire population to that specialty.
- Warriors, magic users and formidable generals appear in **every** kingdom. The actual strongest fighting tradition belongs to the designated Military & Combat kingdom.
- **Education is especially prestigious in the antagonist's royal homeland:** major libraries and stores of knowledge are a defining part of its identity, rather than generic research alone.
- Guild Hunters exist as an institution distinct from (though potentially interacting with) royal armies.
- Education access, academy structures, commoner-vs-nobility privileges, magic registration and rites remain open for later design.
- Religion is **not an intended focus**. Earlier project boundary remains: supernatural phenomena are allowed, but **no real gods/deities or divine-source powers**.

Potential contrasts between the hero's modest upbringing and the antagonist's royal resources should create meaningful social stakes, but exact character biographies remain open.
