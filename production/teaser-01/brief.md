# Teaser 01 — PHASE 1: Working Project Brief

> **Status: DRAFT / AWAITING CREATOR APPROVAL.** This is a working production brief, **not** a finalized story or visual design.  
> Work from [the full pipeline](README.md). Mark this phase complete **only after explicit approval**.

## 1. Goal

Produce a **complete, ~60-second animated teaser** as the creator's first practical production experiment. Use it to discover time, effort and real difficulty across sketch/reference, design, coloring, key poses, animation, compositing, sound and export. Preserve results as evidence for later episode planning.

**Primary use:** learning + technical/visual proof of concept.  
**Public release:** undecided; only release after assets/music/fonts are properly cleared.

## 2. Creative identity

- Original dark, serious, emotionally grounded **fantasy**, medieval-feeling world.
- **World-centric ensemble**, not a Hero/Villain-only advertisement.
- Kingdom landscapes and communities matter; chosen supporting people and NPCs must have visible **purpose/agency**, not function solely as decoration.
- Hero and Antagonist are present, but not required to receive the same duration or most of the total running time.
- Action, normal life, mystery and atmosphere can be mixed with **unequal shot durations** for better rhythm.
- Show details through imagery and action; do not explain full story, reveal all world mysteries or force in every named kingdom.
- All scenes, designs, symbols and character glimpses remain **teaser-only provisional ideas** until confirmed elsewhere.

## 4. What we WILL test

- [ ] Background / kingdom establishing shot
- [ ] At least one meaningful ordinary civilian / NPC / guild or local life moment
- [ ] Selected Hero presence and action
- [ ] Selected Antagonist presence/mystery
- [ ] Camera framing, layered depth and camera movement
- [ ] At least one limited animation movement (gesture, blink, cloth, walk, crowd, etc.)
- [ ] One more challenging hand-drawn sequence, even if only a few seconds
- [ ] Clean coloring and per-shot consistency
- [ ] Ambient/audio layering, editing and final delivery
- [ ] Time and difficulty record for every stage

## 5. What we will NOT attempt by default

- Entire episode or season
- Full canonical screenplay, story ending or detailed lore reveal
- Whole cast, every kingdom, or all geographic locations
- Long continuous fight or 60 seconds of expensive full animation
- Polished models from every angle for every visible NPC
- Expensive tool stack before trying a small test
- Automatic promotion of teaser choices into permanent story canon

## 6. Completion definition

**Phase 1 approved when:**
- [ ] The creator agrees to goal, scope and tone
- [ ] Video format specifications are accepted or revised
- [ ] Whether the teaser is private-only or potentially public-facing is decided
- [ ] Reveal/exclusion boundaries are accepted
- [ ] We can start selecting content in Phase 2 without inventing canon

**Project complete when:** 60-second video delivered, playback QA passes, source material safely retained, a time log is completed, and retrospective records what can improve for the first actual episode.
